# Root & Evergreen — Internal Operational Standards (Enhanced V1)

Root & Evergreen’s operational standards are designed to support sustainable growth, continuity, and a human-centered approach for small businesses. These policies are living documents and will be updated as we learn and grow.

---

## 1. Onboarding Standards

- Every client completes an intake process before active support begins.
- Onboarding gathers:
  - Business overview
  - Employee count
  - Operational goals
  - Company identity/values
  - Current systems/tools
  - Major pain points
- Client folders and continuity structures are created during onboarding.
- A summary of onboarding findings is documented in Google Workspace/Notion.
- Assign a point of contact for questions and provide access to all required tools and documentation.
- Collect feedback on the onboarding process for improvement.

---

## 2. File Organization Standards

- All client information follows standardized folder structures (e.g., Clients, Policies, Templates).
- Templates remain separate from client-specific documents.
- Archived documents are accessible for continuity/history review.
- Files prioritize operational clarity over excessive complexity.
- Name files and folders clearly and consistently.
- Avoid storing business files on personal devices or outside approved platforms.

---

## 3. Response-Time Expectations

- Standard client communication: within 1 business day.
- Escalations or urgent concerns: same-day acknowledgment when possible.
- Internal operational blockers should be communicated immediately.
- If more time is needed, communicate expected timelines.
- Escalate urgent issues per the escalation procedure.

---

## 4. Confidentiality Expectations

- Client information is accessed only by authorized personnel.
- Sensitive operational or employee information remains confidential unless escalation or legal requirements apply.
- AI-generated outputs always receive human review before final client delivery.
- Continuity systems preserve information responsibly and respectfully.
- Store sensitive files in secure, access-controlled folders.
- Report any suspected data breaches or confidentiality concerns immediately.

---

## 5. Escalation Procedures

Escalation should occur when:
- Legal or compliance concerns arise
- Harassment or safety concerns are reported
- Operational breakdowns significantly impact the client
- Account managers are uncertain how to proceed
- Urgent issues require leadership involvement

Escalations should include:
- Summary of issue
- Actions already taken
- Recommended next steps
- Documentation references

Attempt to resolve directly with the involved party first. For urgent or sensitive issues, use the confidential escalation form or contact leadership/HR immediately. Document all steps taken and outcomes.

---

## 6. Account Management Standards

- Each client has a primary account manager whenever possible.
- Account managers are responsible for:
  - Maintaining continuity
  - Updating documentation
  - Coordinating follow-ups
  - Preserving operational clarity
  - Communicating changes clearly
- Handoff documentation is completed whenever account ownership changes.
- Schedule regular check-ins and respond to client needs promptly.
- Use AI tools to support, not replace, human judgment and relationship-building.

---

## 7. AI Usage Standards

AI may assist with:
- Drafting policies
- Generating summaries
- Workflow suggestions
- Documentation organization
- Operational recommendations
- Identifying missing information

AI should NOT replace:
- Human judgment
- Relationship management
- Final approvals
- Escalation decisions
- Confidential oversight responsibilities

All AI-assisted outputs receive human review before being finalized or shared externally. Do not input confidential or sensitive information into AI tools unless approved.

---

## 8. Continuity Documentation Standards

- Key decisions, policy changes, and important actions are documented in shared folders (Google Workspace/Notion).
- Use standardized templates for meeting notes, decisions, and handoffs.
- Documentation is clear, dated, and accessible to relevant staff.
- Updates and changes are logged and versioned.
- Continuity systems ensure organizational memory and smooth operations.

---

## 9. Communication Standards

- All staff communicate respectfully, clearly, and professionally in all channels (in-person, email, chat, etc.).
- Use plain language and avoid jargon.
- Address disagreements constructively and privately.
- Offensive, discriminatory, or aggressive language is not permitted.
- Feedback is specific, actionable, and focused on work, not personal traits.
- Communication is timely and acknowledges receipt when appropriate.

---

## 10. Operational Philosophy

Root & Evergreen is designed to:
- Support sustainable organizational growth
- Reduce operational fragmentation
- Preserve continuity
- Maintain human-centered communication
- Create scalable operational clarity without unnecessary corporate complexity
- Encourage feedback and continuous improvement

---

*These standards are reviewed regularly and updated as Root & Evergreen grows. Feedback is always welcome.*
