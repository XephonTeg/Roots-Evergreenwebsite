# Root & Evergreen — Internal Operational Standards (V1)

## Onboarding Standards

* Every client should complete an intake process before active support begins.
* Initial onboarding should gather:
  * business overview
  * employee count
  * operational goals
  * company identity/values
  * current systems/tools
  * major pain points
* Client folders and continuity structures should be created during onboarding.
* A summary of onboarding findings should be documented.

## File Organization Standards

* All client information should follow standardized folder structures.
* Templates should remain separate from client-specific documents.
* Archived documents should remain accessible for continuity/history review.
* Files should prioritize operational clarity over excessive complexity.

## Response-Time Expectations

* Standard client communication: within 1 business day
* Escalations or urgent concerns: same-day acknowledgment when possible
* Internal operational blockers should be communicated immediately

## Confidentiality Expectations

* Client information should only be accessed by authorized personnel.
* Sensitive operational or employee information should remain confidential unless escalation or legal requirements apply.
* AI-generated outputs should always receive human review before final client delivery.
* Continuity systems should preserve information responsibly and respectfully.

## Escalation Procedures

Escalation should occur when:

* legal or compliance concerns arise
* harassment or safety concerns are reported
* operational breakdowns significantly impact the client
* account managers are uncertain how to proceed
* urgent issues require leadership involvement

Escalations should include:

* summary of issue
* actions already taken
* recommended next steps
* documentation references

## Account Management Standards

* Each client should have a primary account manager whenever possible.
* Account managers are responsible for:
  * maintaining continuity
  * updating documentation
  * coordinating follow-ups
  * preserving operational clarity
  * communicating changes clearly
* Handoff documentation should be completed whenever account ownership changes.

## AI Usage Standards

AI may assist with:

* drafting policies
* generating summaries
* workflow suggestions
* documentation organization
* operational recommendations
* identifying missing information

AI should NOT replace:

* human judgment
* relationship management
* final approvals
* escalation decisions
* confidential oversight responsibilities

All AI-assisted outputs should receive human review before being finalized or shared externally.

## Operational Philosophy

Root & Evergreen is designed to:

* support sustainable organizational growth
* reduce operational fragmentation
* preserve continuity
* maintain human-centered communication
* create scalable operational clarity without unnecessary corporate complexity
