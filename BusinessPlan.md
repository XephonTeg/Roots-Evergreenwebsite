# Root & Evergreen Business Plan (V1)

## Executive Summary
Root & Evergreen is a human-centered HR and business operations support company designed for small businesses that need professional HR and operational systems without building a full internal department. We combine high-touch service with AI-supported account managers to deliver scalable, personalized solutions that prioritize community, continuity, and sustainable growth.

## Mission & Values
- **Mission:** Empower small businesses with trustworthy, growth-oriented, and supportive HR and operational systems that foster long-term success.
- **Values:** Trustworthy, Growth-oriented, Grounded, Supportive, Scalable, Professional (without being overly corporate)

## Market Analysis & Ideal Customer
- **Target Market:** Small businesses and growing organizations (10–200 employees) without a full HR department.
- **Pain Points:** Lack of HR expertise, compliance risk, inefficient onboarding, policy gaps, operational continuity challenges.
- **Competitive Advantage:** Human-centered, community-first approach; scalable systems; AI-supported account managers; continuity and legacy focus.

## Service Offerings
### Core Launch Services
- HR support
- Onboarding/offboarding
- Policy generation and management
- Employee handbooks
- Workplace harassment procedures
- Job descriptions
- Orientation systems
- Performance reviews
- Filing organization
- Basic compliance guidance
- Continuity & legacy systems

### Future/Secondary Services
- Payroll/benefits/401k coordination
- Scheduling and task management
- Event coordination
- Training systems
- Community/business networking
- Operational dashboards

## Operational Workflows & Systems
- **Client Intake:** Discovery, onboarding, and needs assessment
- **Account Management:** Dedicated manager, regular check-ins, AI-supported resource surfacing
- **Policy Management:** Creation, review, approval, versioning, and communication
- **Notifications:** Automated/manual reminders, escalation, and tracking
- **Continuity & Legacy:** Centralized documentation, decision logs, handoff protocols
- **Organizational Learning:** Feedback loops, best practice sharing, knowledge base
- **Employee Consideration:** Plain-language policies, easy access, feedback collection

## Go-to-Market & Client Acquisition
- Direct outreach to small business networks and associations
- Referrals from existing clients and partners
- Educational content and webinars
- Partnerships with local business support organizations
- Early adopter incentives and testimonials

## Team & Roles
- Founder/Director
- Account Managers (AI-supported)
- Operations/Compliance Specialist (as needed)
- Administrative Support (as needed)

## Technology & Tooling Stack
- **Google Workspace:** File storage, docs, sheets, forms, email, calendar
- **Notion:** Knowledge base, continuity files, SOPs, policies, onboarding docs
- **ChatGPT/Claude:** Strategy, workflow refinement, technical planning, documentation
- **(Future) Airtable:** Workflow and client status tracking, dashboards

## Financial Projections (V1)
- **Revenue:** Service-based, monthly retainer or per-project fees
- **Expenses:** Account manager compensation, software subscriptions, marketing, admin
- **Growth Plan:** Start lean, validate demand, acquire first 5–10 clients, refine systems, expand team and services as needed

## Milestones & Next Steps
- Finalize operational workflows and templates
- Set up internal workspace and tools
- Launch pilot with first clients
- Collect feedback and refine services
- Apply for grants and funding
- Develop marketing and outreach materials
- Plan for phased automation and scaling

## Grant Alignment & Impact
Root & Evergreen addresses a critical gap for small businesses by providing affordable, scalable HR and operational support. Our approach fosters local economic growth, supports job creation, and preserves organizational knowledge. Grant funding will accelerate our ability to serve more businesses, develop training resources, and build sustainable, community-oriented systems.
